\textit{The content of this chapter is based on the following published journal article:}

\textbf{Khan, M. A. H.; Tait, K. N.; Derwent, R. G.; Roome, S.; Bacak, A.; Bullock, S.; Lowenberg, M. H.; Shallcross, D. E.}, "Off-setting climate change through formation flying of aircraft, a feasibility study reliant on high fidelity gas-phase chemical kinetic data", \textit{Internation Journal of Chemical Kinetics}, vol. 55, no. 7, pp. 402--412, 2023. DOI: \url{https://doi.org/10.1002/kin.21644}.

This chapter incorporates material from that work with permission from the publisher and co-authors, where necessary. Dr Anwar Khan was lead author, and together with Prof. Dudley Shallcross, provided the theory and methodology for the study, and conducted the global chemistry analysis using STOCHEM-CRI. Prof. Richard Derwent developed and implemented the 1D box model approach, using BOXM v1.0 photochemical trajectory model \cite{Derwent2010}. I co-wrote the paper with Dr Khan, Prof. Shallcross and Prof. Derwent, carrying out further analysis and validation using the In-service Aircraft for a Global Observing System (IAGOS) dataset. The remaining authors contributed in supporting roles.
