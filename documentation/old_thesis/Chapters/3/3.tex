\textit{The content of this chapter is based on the following published conference article:}

\textbf{Tait, K. N.; Roome, S.; Hoole, J.}, ``Investigating the Real-World Feasibility of Aircraft Exhaust Plume Intersections using Open Source Air Traffic Data'', \textit{Engineering Proceedings}, vol. 28, no. 1, pp. 16, 2022. DOI: \url{https://doi.org/10.3390/proceedings1010000}.

This chapter incorporates material from that work with permission from the publisher and co-authors, where necessary. I was lead author of this publication; I wrote the software code and conducted the simulation study with Stephen Roome, and wrote the paper in cooperation with Dr Josh Hoole (University of Bristol, Dept. of Aerospace Engineering). I also travelled to the Delft University of Technology, Netherlands, to present this research at the OpenSky Symposium 2022 \cite{Sun2022}.

This paper and associated research naturally followed on from the literature review publication that formed chapter \ref{chapter2}. Throughout the process of reviewing the literature, it became apparent that only a handful of papers have investigated the prospect of aircraft plume overlap in real-world airspace (e.g. \cite{Schlager1997}).
