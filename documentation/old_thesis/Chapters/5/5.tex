In this chapter, a robust methodology is presented for estimating the chemical saturation effects that occur, as a result of overlapping aircraft exhaust plumes in busy airspace. This is done through explicitly modelling aircraft trajectories, simulating their flight performance to ascertain fuel burn and emissions data, then simulating plume dispersion to investigate how those emissions mix into the surrounding atmosphere. Furthermore, these data are aggregated to a common Eulerian grid for multiple flights in the same region, enabling further chemical and microphysical modelling, to explore a range of responses to aircraft emissions in high-density airspace.
