\section{Parametric Study}
\begin{equation}
    delO3_nac = O3 - O3_bg
    delO3_sat = n_ac * delO3_1ac - delO3_nac
    S = delO3_sat / n_ac
\end{equation}

S is the saturation factor. This is how much ppb is reduced per aircraft in the scenario. Indicates degree of saturation, normalised to the number of aircraft in the study.
